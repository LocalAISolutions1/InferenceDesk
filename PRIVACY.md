# InferenceDesk Privacy Policy  
**Company:** Local AI Solutions (“Local AI Solutions,” “we,” “us”)  
**Product:** InferenceDesk Desktop Application and related services (the “App” / “Services”)  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  

This Privacy Policy explains how Local AI Solutions collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the InferenceDesk App and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, do not use the App.

---

## 1) Key Idea: Local-First
InferenceDesk is designed to run inference locally on your computer. In typical use:

- Your prompts, files, and model outputs stay on your device.
- We do **not** receive or store your prompts, files, or model outputs as part of normal use of the App.
- We do not receive your prompts or model outputs unless you explicitly choose a feature that sends data to us (for example, sending a support bundle you generate).
- Some features may connect to the internet (for example: update checks, downloading runtimes, browsing/downloading models, downloading the Harmony framework, optional sign-in, or license verification for paid features). This Policy describes those cases.

## 2) Information We Collect

### A. Information you provide directly
We may collect information you provide when you:
- Contact support (e.g., email address and the content of your message)
- Submit feedback
- Choose to send diagnostic logs or a support bundle
- Choose to create and use an optional account (if available)
- Purchase paid features (if/when available)

### B. Information processed locally on your device
The App may process locally:
- Prompts, chats, text, files, and other inputs you load into the App
- Outputs produced by models
- Model configuration, settings, and local session history (if you enable it)

This information is stored on your device (where applicable). We do not automatically collect it.

### C. Technical data (limited) from network features
When the App connects to the internet (for example, checking for updates, downloading app updates/runtimes, downloading the Harmony framework, browsing/downloading models from third-party services, or using optional sign-in), our servers and/or service providers may receive basic technical information such as:
- IP address
- Timestamp of the request
- Requested URL/resource
- User-agent / app version (if sent)
- Basic error codes

This is common for hosting and content delivery and is generally required to operate downloads/updates securely and prevent abuse.

**Service providers that may receive these requests include:**
- Cloudflare (hosting/content delivery for runtimes and downloads)
- Hugging Face (model browsing and downloads, if you use those features)
- Firebase (authentication, if you choose to sign in)

### D. Diagnostics / Telemetry
We do **not** collect telemetry or crash reports at this time.

If we introduce optional diagnostics in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where feasible, provide controls in the App.

### E. Account data (optional sign-in)
The App may offer optional sign-up/sign-in using a third-party authentication provider (currently Firebase Authentication). If you choose to create or use an account, the authentication provider may process:
- Email address (used for sign-in and account recovery)
- A unique account identifier (UID)
- Authentication metadata (such as sign-in timestamps and security-related information)

If you continue as a guest and do not sign in, account information is not collected through the sign-in feature.
